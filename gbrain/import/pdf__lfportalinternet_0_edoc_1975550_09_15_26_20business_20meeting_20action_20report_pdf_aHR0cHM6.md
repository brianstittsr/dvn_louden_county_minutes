   14.j    LEGI-2025-0007, Tech Park at Dulles Substation: SPEX-2025-0016 & SPEX-
           2025-0020
           Election District(s): Dulles
           Staff Contact(s): Darby Metcalf & Daniel Galindo, Planning and Zoning
           Supervisor Letourneau moved that the Board of Supervisors deny LEGI-
           2025-0007, Tech Park at Dulles Substation: SPEX- 2025-0016 & SPEX-2025-
           0020, based on the following Findings for Denial:

               •   The existing use, a fully developed vehicle auction facility, is consistent
                   with the Suburban Industrial/Mineral Extraction Place Type of the
                   Loudoun County 2019 General Plan and is a reasonably economically
                   viable use.

               •   The proposed utility substation use would require conditions to mitigate
                   its impacts, but the existing use is consistent with the surrounding
                   development, on a scale more compatible with the location, and does
                   not require conditions to mitigate its impacts.

               •   The electrical grid in Loudoun County is severely strained due to
                   increased electrical demand and constraints on delivering sufficient
                   power to the County. The proposed utility substation is a new direct
                   connect substation, as opposed to a reliability substation, which will
                   exacerbate the problem by promoting development of new high
                   wattage users and causing off site impacts of new high voltage
                   transmission lines within the County to support such new users.
           (Seconded by Chair Randall. The motion passed 6-3: Supervisors Glass,
           Kershner, and Umstattd opposed.)
