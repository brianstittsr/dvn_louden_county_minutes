                      Data Centers in Loudoun County
                                                  Stay Informed. Get Involved.
                                                                         Information for Residents
Data center development is an important issue for all Loudoun County residents. This resource provides
information and actionable steps for residents in relation to Loudoun County’s data centers.

How is Loudoun County Addressing Concerns About Data Centers?
  In 2025, the Board of Supervisors (BOS) ended by-right development of data centers. All new data
  center development requires legislative approval from the BOS.
  Loudoun County is updating the 2019 Comprehensive Plan and Zoning Ordinance to:
      Improve the county’s ability to influence and guide the location, development and management of
      high-voltage transmission corridors and electrical infrastructure; and
      Identify and mitigate adverse impacts of data centers through ordinances and amendments around
      noise, screening, standards for generators and siting of other components of data centers.
  The Board of Supervisors and county staff are hosting town hall events and public engagement
  meetings to hear residents’ concerns and to provide information about data center development.
