       Moratorium on Data Centers

• July 22, 2026: Seconded motion made by Supervisor Briskman to pause
  legislative data center applications and substations until new regulations are
  adopted with the approval of Data Center Standards, Phase 2.
• September 15, 2026: Motion was approved 7-1-1 to pause legislative
  applications for data centers and substations for 12 months from the date it was
  first submitted to the Planning Commission. During the pause, Board may take
  up applications that involve a grid reliability substation.
• October 20, 2026: Final vote on pause/moratorium resolution.
