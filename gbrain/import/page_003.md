Phase 2: Data Center Standards & Locations # Phase 2: Data Center Standards & Locations ## Public Engagement: Open Houses Set for Sept. 28 and Sept. 30 Members of the public may participate in open house-style events taking place from 6:00 to 8:30 p.m. on the following dates. - **Monday, September 28, 2026****,** Broad Run High School cafeteria, 21670 Ashburn Road, Ashburn. - **Wednesday, September 30, 2026,** Douglass Community Center main gym, 407 E Market St., Leesburg The open house events will be identical in format and information presented, so attendees need only come to one. Unlike a traditional public meeting or town hall, the open houses will not feature formal remarks or a presentation. The events are designed as drop-in open houses. Attendees may come and go anytime between 6:00 and 8:30 p.m. Opportunities for engagement at each event will include personal conversations with staff from the Loudoun County Department of Planning and Zoning, brief online surveys or polls, and noise metering demonstrations from county Zoning Enforcement. Find details in [this news release](https://www.loudoun.gov/m/NewsFlash/home/detail/10823). - [Five Things to Know Before You Go to the Data Center Standards and Locations Open Houses](https://www.loudoun.gov/Blog.aspx?IID=1577#item) ## Data Center Standards and Locations Phase 2 On September 16, 2025, the Loudoun County Board of Supervisors approved the Data Center Standards and Locations Phase 2 Project Plan. Phase 2 is intended to identify and mitigate anticipated data center compatibility issues through the Special Exception (SPEX) process. Phase 2 will consider evolving industry operational needs and seek to appropriately mitigate adverse impacts without unduly burdening existing data centers or hindering appropriate redevelopment and reinvestment. The project will focus on Comprehensive Plan changes that address emerging industry needs, assess guidance for evaluating data centers as a conditional use, address utility substation definition and policy, consider energy opportunities and clean up language to align with the approval of [Phase 1](https://www.loudoun.gov/6221/Phase-1-Project-Plan-for-Data-Center-Sta). Staff will also review and potentially revisit some use-specific standards for data centers and utility substations, review building height rules, onsite power generation and energy storage, noise, parking and the deletion of the Urban Employment Zoning District. This project is not anticipated to consider changes to the Loudoun County Comprehensive Plan maps or the zoning maps. ## Public Comment Members of the public may provide comments to Department of Planning and Zoning staff at any time using the online comment form below.
