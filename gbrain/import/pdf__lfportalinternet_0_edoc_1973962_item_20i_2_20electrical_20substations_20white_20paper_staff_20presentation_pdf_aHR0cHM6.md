Recommended Definition Changes
Update “Electrical Substation” Definition:
• A facility consisting of electrical equipment and associated
  structures used to transform voltage, switch, regulate,
  control, convert, or interconnect electric power within a
  transmission or distribution system, or between the electric
  grid and a specific user or group of users. Substations may
  be owned by a utility or a customer and may include air-
  insulated, gas-insulated, indoor, outdoor, switching,
  converter, or collector substations.




                                                                 10

Recommended Definition Changes
Add Definitions:
• Air-Insulated Substation: an electrical substation in which
  ambient air at atmospheric pressure serves as the primary
  insulating medium between energized components and
  between energized equipment and ground. Electrical
  equipment is installed outdoors or in open structures with
  physical clearances sized to prevent electrical flashover.
• Gas-Insulated Substation: an electrical substation in which
  pressurized insulating gas is used as the primary insulating
  medium, with conductors and switching equipment enclosed
  within sealed, grounded metal housings, allowing significantly
  reduced clearances and compact substation layouts.

Conclusions
• Substations are critical to grid reliability and vary significantly
  in scale, technology, and community impact
• The County’s definition of “Utility Substation” should be
  updated to reflect modern substation types
• Effective siting requires consideration of technical,
  operational, and land-use factors
• Screening and buffering can help mitigate visual impacts,
  while GIS and architectural enclosures can reduce overall site
  area
• Aligning land-use regulations with current industry practices
  will support infrastructure needs and community compatibility
