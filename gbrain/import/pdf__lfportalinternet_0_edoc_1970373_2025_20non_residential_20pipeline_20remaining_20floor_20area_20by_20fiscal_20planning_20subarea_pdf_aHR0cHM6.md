      Non-Residential Land Use Category Definitions:
      Agriculture/Agritourism: Includes agriculture and agriculture-related uses (such as agriculture support and services, agricultural processing, agricultural cultural centers, agricultural research facility, agritourism and agritainment).
      Commercial: Buildings or land used for sales of merchandise at retail or for the rendering of personal services.
      Community/Religious: Uses providing meeting areas for religious observances and activities (including churches, synagogues, temples or mosques), private health, recreational or educational (such as parks, schools, libraries, hospitals and camps), museums, stadiums, funeral homes, private community
      centers, and private child care centers.
      Data Centers: A facility used primarily for the storage, management, processing, and transmission of digital data, which houses computer and/or network equipment, systems, servers, appliances and other associated components related to digital data operations.
      Government/Institutional: Public uses such as public meeting halls, libraries, art galleries or museums, post office, and like uses. Public health, recreational or educational uses such as parks, schools, libraries, hospitals and camps. Institutional uses include public or private hospitals, colleges and universities.
      Hotel: Any single building or group of dwelling units, combined or separated, used for the purpose of housing transient guests.
      Industrial: Non-residential and non-commercial employment uses such as, but not limited to, light and heavy manufacturing, warehousing and logistics, manufacturing/warehousing, research & development, mining, and milling.
      Office: Any room, studio, clinic, suite or building wherein the primary use is the conduct of a business. Category includes commercial and government office space, as well as flex industrial office space. An office shall not involve manufacturing, fabrication, production, processing, assembling, cleaning, testing,
      repair or storage of materials, goods and products; or the sale and/or delivery of any materials, goods or products which are physically located on the premises. An office shall not be deemed to include a veterinary service or animal hospital.
      Utilities & Infrastructure: Facilities for the provision of infrastructure services that support legally established uses and that need to be located in or near the area where the service is provided. Services may be publicly or privately provided and include structured parking.

      The remaining non-residential floor area is presented in terms of the remaining Legislatively Approved Floor Area and the remaining Site Planned Floor Area. The Remaining Legislatively Approved Floor Area represents square footage approved through legislative approvals, such as rezonings and zoning
      concept plan amendments, minus the floor area already built. Remaining Site Planned Floor Area represents floor area approved to be built through administrative, by-right site plans, minus the floor area already built.

      Typically, development projects may have a large floor area approved by legislative action, but build out that approved floor area gradually over time. Site planned floor area may represent only a portion of the area approved by legislative action. However, not all legislatively approved floor area is always
      built. Presenting both types of remaining floor area can give a better overall sense of the remaining floor area to be built in a given project or area.
