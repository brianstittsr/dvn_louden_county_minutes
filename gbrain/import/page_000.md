Data Center Standards & Locations # Data Center Standards & Locations ## News and Announcements: Open Houses Scheduled Loudoun County will hold two open house events in connection with phase two of the Data Center Standards and Locations planning and zoning project in September 2026. The open houses, which will be identical in format and information presented, will take place from 6:00 to 8:30 p.m. on the following dates: - **Monday, September 28**, Broad Run High School cafeteria, 21670 Ashburn Road, Ashburn - **Wednesday, September 30**, Douglass Community Center main gym, 407 E Market St., Leesburg Unlike a traditional public meeting or town hall, the open houses will not feature remarks or a presentation. The events are designed as drop-in open houses, in which attendees may come and go anytime between 6:00 and 8:30 p.m. Opportunities for engagement at each event will include personal conversations with staff from the Loudoun County Department of Planning and Zoning, brief online surveys or polls, noise metering demonstrations from county Zoning Enforcement and more. - [Five Things to Know Before You Go to the Data Center Standards and Locations Open Houses](https://www.loudoun.gov/Blog.aspx?IID=1577#item) Input collected at these events will inform staff recommendations when the project comes before the Planning Commission, which is expected to occur in February 2027. Members of the public are welcome to provide comments on the Data Center Standards and Locations project at any time via [this online comment form](https://www.loudoun.gov/FormCenter/Planning-and-Zoning-19/Data-Center-Standards-and-Locations-Phas-496). ## Project Information On February 6, 2024, the Loudoun County Board of Supervisors initiated a project to review and update policies and standards for data center and electrical substation uses. On July 2, 2024, the Board approved an Intent to Amend the Zoning Ordinance and the Revised Project Plan for Data Center Standards and Locations (CPAM-2024-0001 and ZOAM-2024-0001), which separated the project into two phases. - **Phase 1**, approved on March 18, 2025, focused on proposed map and text amendments to the Loudoun County 2019 Comprehensive Plan, and amendments to the Loudoun County Zoning Ordinance, to designate data centers as a conditional or Special Exception (SPEX) use in areas where the uses were previously allowed by-right. - **Phase 2,** now underway, will focus on establishing new potential policy guidance and new use-specific zoning standards for data centers and utility substations. ### Grandfathering Resolution The Board also adopted a Grandfathering Resolution. This resolution outlines rules under which certain data center applications accepted prior to February 12, 2025, may continue to be reviewed and processed without obtaining SPEX approval for a data center use. - [Read the adopted Grandfathering Resolution (PDF)](https://www.loudoun.gov/DocumentCenter/View/215835). ### Application Pathway Guide: Understanding the Data Center ZOAM and Grandfathering Resolution To help applicants navigate the new Zoning Ordinance provisions and Grandfathering Resolution, the county has developed a resource intended to clarify how the ZOAM and the Grandfathering Resolution should be applied. It outlines key decision points and scenarios to assist applicants in determining whether their applications will need to obtain a SPEX approval for a data center use. - [Read the Application Pathway Guide (PDF)](https://www.loudoun.gov/DocumentCenter/View/215836). *Please note:* The guide is an illustrative tool. The specific outcome for any project will depend on its individual circumstances. In the event of any discrepancy, the Loudoun County Zoning Ordinance and the adopted Grandfathering Resolution prevail over the Application Pathway Guide. ## Public Comment Members of the public may provide comments to Department of Planning and Zoning staff at any time using the online comment form below.
